# Privacy Policy

**Goalie Mom**  
**Effective date:** October 26, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Goalie Mom (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Goalie Mom iOS app and the website at [goaliemom.app](https://www.goaliemom.app) (together, the “Services”).

## 1. Who we are

**App / website:** Goalie Mom  
**Contact:** goaliemomapp@gmail.com  
**Website:** https://www.goaliemom.app

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you enter in the app
The app lets you track goalie and team performance. You may enter:
- Goalie, team, and season names
- Game names, dates, home/away status
- Shots, goals, attempted shots, and notes
- Other stats and notes you choose to record

This information is stored **locally on your device** using Apple’s SwiftData. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this game data.

### Information you send us
If you contact us (for example via the in-app Email Support button or by emailing goaliemomapp@gmail.com), we receive the content of your message and your email address.

### Subscription and purchase information
Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details. We receive limited information from Apple about whether you have an active subscription so we can unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

### Device and technical information (app)
The app may access basic device information (such as device model and iOS version) when you send a support email, so we can help troubleshoot. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the current version of the app.

### Website analytics
Our website uses **Google Analytics via the Site Kit plugin**. Google Analytics collects information about how visitors use the site, including:
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Referring site or source
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Device and browser type
- Other standard usage data

Google may use cookies or similar technologies to collect this information. You can learn more about how Google uses data at [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and opt out of Google Analytics tracking with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:
- Provide the core tracking and stats features of the app
- Process and manage subscriptions via Apple
- Respond to support requests
- Understand how the website is used and improve it
- Improve and maintain the Services
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Sharing of information

We share information only in these limited cases:
- **Apple** — to process in-app purchases and subscriptions, and to distribute the app.
- **Google** — for website analytics via Google Analytics (Site Kit).
- **Email provider** — when you contact us at goaliemomapp@gmail.com.
- **Hosting provider** — for the website.
- **As required by law** — if we are legally obligated to disclose information.

Game and stats data you enter in the app stays on your device unless you choose to share it (for example, using the app’s share feature, which uses the iOS share sheet).

## 5. Children’s privacy

Goalie Mom is intended for use by adults (such as parents or coaches) to track youth hockey games. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Game data is entered by the adult user.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- Game and tracking data is stored on your device. You can delete individual games or all games for a context from within the app, or delete the app to remove all local data.
- Support emails are retained as long as needed to respond to your request and for our records.
- Subscription status is managed by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.
- Website analytics data is retained by Google according to your Google Analytics settings (typically 14 months by default, configurable in the Google Analytics property).

## 7. Your choices

- **Local data:** You control the game data stored on your device. Delete games in the app or delete the app.
- **Subscriptions:** Manage or cancel in Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or through the App Store.
- **Support communications:** You can stop emailing us at any time. We will delete support correspondence upon reasonable request where we are not required to retain it.
- **Website analytics:** Use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or adjust your browser’s cookie settings. Note that the app itself does not use Google Analytics.

## 8. Security

We rely on Apple’s platform security for data stored on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information we handle.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
